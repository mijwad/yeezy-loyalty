User Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Meta Platform Policies and global data privacy standards (GDPR, DPDP), this page explains how diners and users can request permanent deletion of their personal information from BeeBill.

How to Request Data Deletion

BeeBill provides two simple and automated ways to erase your personal contact details, dining history, and loyalty profile:

Method 1: Instant WhatsApp Opt-Out & Erasure (Recommended)

If you received a digital bill or loyalty notification on WhatsApp from BeeBill, simply reply with the keyword STOP directly in the chat.

Our automated webhook will immediately opt out your phone number from all subsequent communications and flag your profile for permanent contact dissociation.

Method 2: Email Data Erasure Request

You can send an email to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@beebill.online with the following details:

  • Subject line: "Request for Personal Data Deletion"
  • Body: Specify your mobile phone number (with country code, e.g. +91XXXXXXXXXX) and your name.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon receiving and verifying a deletion request, we will permanently purge:

  • Your phone number, diner name, and demographic identifiers from our active loyalty member directories.
  • Your accumulated reward points and loyalty balance.
  • Direct contact mappings in our messaging delivery queue.

Note: Anonymized transaction records required for tax audit compliance and statutory accounting will be retained in accordance with local financial regulations.

Processing Timeline

All verified deletion requests received via email are processed within 48 to 72 business hours. You will receive a written confirmation email once the deletion is finalized.
